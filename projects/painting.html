Paintings

Left: Childhood Toys Still Life. I chose to paint a Rubik’s cube and kendama for this project not only because they are awesome toys, but because they also embody my playful, puzzle-solving spirit.

Middle: Extents & Degrees. For this project, I was limited to using a grid with uniform color swatches. I decided to use a primary triad, which was inspired by the color scheme of Pieter de Hooch's "The Bedroom" painting.

Right: Covering Style. This is a book cover for a fictional book highlighting the works of Zaha Hadid. On the cover, I painted a chair she designed for her UltraStellar furniture collection, and both of the typefaces I used for lettering were designed by her.

Medium
Still life — acrylic paint on canvas. Extents & Degrees and Covering Style — acrylic paint on museum board.
Dimensions
Still life — 12" × 18", Extents & Degrees — 16" × 10", Covering Style — 16" × 10".
