Technical Arts

This is an original design for a gecko-inspired speaker that I made in my Circuits and Electronics: Analog and Digital class. It includes a printed circuit board that I designed using Autodesk’s EAGLE program. It also includes a Bluetooth module and a potentiometer that controls volume. I did all the soldering on my printed circuit board and burned myself only a few times. The light feature is made using the Adafruit Circuit Playground, and I programmed it to display a pattern that changes brightness depending on how loud the speakers are.

Medium
Wood MDF board, acrylic, custom electronics.
Dimensions
10" × 16" × 3".

This is an original design for a lamp I built in my Design and Technology class. The idea of using cubic shapes for the light enclosure came to me as I was looking at a stack of Rubik’s cubes on my desk, and I was inspired to make the base of the lamp look like bubbles to contrast the cubic light enclosure. The lights run on an Arduino board that I programmed to flash specific patterns, too.

Medium
Wood, metal, acrylic, custom electronics.
Dimensions
6" × 16" × 10".
