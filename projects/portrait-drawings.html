Portrait Drawings

Justice and community

My grandfather Chang-Lin Tien’s kind smile reminds me to stand for justice with compassion. The grit in Sharbat Gula’s expression inspires me to advocate for vulnerable and underprivileged people. Robert F. Kennedy’s words about challenge and opportunity connect this series to the pursuit of justice.

My first name, Justin, is derived from the Latin word justus, and my Chinese middle name, “Zunyi,” translates to respect justice. In my art and design, I aim to improve the community by addressing economic, environmental, and social issues through contemporary designs. My ultimate dream is to nurture ubudehe, the Rwandan practice of building by the community for the community. These figures inspire me to persist in the search for socially responsible practices while advocating for vulnerable and underprivileged people around the world.

“All of us might wish at times that we lived in a more tranquil world, but we don’t. And if our times are difficult and perplexing, so are they challenging and filled with opportunity.”

Robert F. Kennedy
