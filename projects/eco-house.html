Eco House

This original design, inspired by the works of James Cutler, is an eco-friendly house located on Yerba Buena Island in the San Francisco Bay. It was an in-class project. The clients in our program were a couple: a musician and an artist. They requested a performance hall, music studio, art studio, sculpture garden, and living area. Some of the eco-friendly features on the house include deciduous trees in front of the south-facing double-paned windows, rammed earth walls, and being built into the hill on the site for improved insulation, reducing the energy expended on climate control.

Medium
Model — 2-ply, balsa wood, foam core, foliage, tacky glue. Floor plans and section — ink on vellum, Prisma colored pencils.
Dimensions
Model — 12" × 20" × 5". Floor plans and section — 20" × 16".
