Tiny Houses

My accessory dwelling unit is an original design inspired by the works of Swatt Miers Architects. Swatt Miers’ principal design philosophy uses minimalist, modernist vocabulary with a palette of materials that make the building look unimposing and welcoming. My portable home is an original design to accommodate two adults. It is designed to fit on a 26′ × 8.5′ flatbed chassis. It includes a kitchenette, small bathroom, dining area, living area, and a sleeping loft.
