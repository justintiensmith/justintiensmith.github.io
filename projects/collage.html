Collage

Left: My Galactic Warmth Quilt was inspired by my love for the movie "Star Wars." I made the quilt with the guidance of my older sister, who is an avid seamstress, and it won a first-place award at the San Mateo County Fair.

Right: My self-portrait collage was made in my contemporary media and art class. I chose to use blues and greens because they represent the color scheme of our planet. It is a reflection of my commitment to preserving the natural environment.

Medium
Quilt — fabric. Self-portrait — construction paper, magazines.
Dimensions
Quilt — 5' × 6'. Self-portrait — 10" × 14".
